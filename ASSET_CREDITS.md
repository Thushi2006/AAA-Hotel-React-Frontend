# Assets and design references

All property imagery is **illustrative stock content**, not documentation of the client’s hotel. Replace it with client-approved photographs/footage before public release. No video or graphics were copied from the two design references.

## Design references reviewed

- Antyra hospitality/web portfolio: https://www.antyrasolutions.com/website-design-and-development/
- Kōzōwood: https://kozowood.com/en

Inspiration: warm earth tones, generous editorial typography, cinematic imagery, purposeful motion and a calm visual hierarchy. The AAA layout, copy and monogram were created for this project.
